# Conversation Script — Morning in the Orchard Garden

- **Target length:** about 2-3 minutes on camera (10 turns, 5 per speaker).
- **Before filming:** read the filming guide — [../conversation-filming-protocol.md](../conversation-filming-protocol.md) — and print the cue card.
- **Turn-taking:** one speaker at a time, always. Finish your turn, then stay silent; the other speaker waits **1 to 1.5 seconds** before starting. Never overlap, never talk over each other.
- **Do not improvise names or numbers** — the script has none on purpose.

---

**SPEAKER A:** The wind was so strong last night that I could not stop thinking about the garden. I got up very early this morning and went outside to check on everything.

**SPEAKER B:** I heard the wind as well, and I was up before the light came. A few of the flowers are down on the ground, but most of them look really good.

**SPEAKER A:** That is good news, because I was worried about them all night. We should water the whole garden this morning, because the soil looks very hard. The flowers will need our help after a night like that.

**SPEAKER B:** I can water the flowers near the house, and you can do the far part of the garden. After that, we should bring all the food inside before the people come.

**SPEAKER A:** Good plan — some people are coming by this morning to look around the garden. They always ask so many questions, so think about what you will tell them.

**SPEAKER B:** I enjoy talking with the people who come to look around. Last week a woman asked me about the soil and the water, and she wanted to know everything.

**SPEAKER A:** Next week I hope to put new flowers in the ground, and we will need more hands. Do you think your sisters can come out and help for a few hours?

**SPEAKER B:** They can come, and they always enjoy working outside in the garden. My dad might come as well, because he really likes being out in the wind and the light.

**SPEAKER A:** Then we will have all the help we need for the week. When the work is done, we can all share some good food together outside.

**SPEAKER B:** That sounds wonderful to me, so let me get the water going now. You can tell everyone the plan, and I think this will be a very good week for the garden.
