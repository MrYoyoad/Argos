# Conversation Script — A Full Morning

- **Target length:** about 2-3 minutes on camera (10 turns, 5 per speaker).
- **Before filming:** read the filming guide — [../conversation-filming-protocol.md](../conversation-filming-protocol.md) — and print the cue card.
- **Turn-taking:** one speaker at a time, always. Finish your turn, then stay silent; the other speaker waits **1 to 1.5 seconds** before starting. Never overlap, never talk over each other.
- **Do not improvise names or numbers** — the script has none on purpose.

---

**SPEAKER A:** I got up very early this morning because I have so much to do before work. Do you have a big morning as well, or can you stay home for a while?

**SPEAKER B:** My morning is a long one, because I need to go to the office for a few hours. Then my family is coming to the house, and I should make everything look good.

**SPEAKER A:** That is wonderful — is your whole family coming, or just your sisters? They always bring so much food when they come to your house.

**SPEAKER B:** My dad and my sisters are coming this time, and they are staying for the night. We will talk for hours, because we have not been together for such a long time.

**SPEAKER A:** What will you make for them when they come? You always make such good food, and your family knows that very well.

**SPEAKER B:** I have not thought about that so far, but maybe something they know and like. My dad enjoys everything, so he is never the problem — my sisters ask for more.

**SPEAKER A:** And how are things going for you at work this week? When we talked before, everything at the office felt really hard for you.

**SPEAKER B:** Work is going much better now, because we have a new person in the office. She helps me with the hard parts, and I feel much better about my job.

**SPEAKER A:** That is really good to hear after such a hard time. Come by after your family leaves, and tell me all about the night.

**SPEAKER B:** I will do that, and you can tell me about your morning as well. Have a good time at work, and say hello to everyone for me.
