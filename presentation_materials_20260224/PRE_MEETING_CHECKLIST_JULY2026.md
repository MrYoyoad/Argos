## 🟡 SIX QUESTIONS TO ASK IN THE MEETING (sticky note)

1. "What does **good enough** look like for your workflow — gist/triage, or verbatim?"
2. "Can you film **native-res, frontal, closer**? Shall we run the **re-shoot pilot** together?"
3. "Is **multi-speaker** still your top capability gap?" (the scoped project is ready)
4. "**Which Arabic** — MSA, Levantine, Egyptian, Gulf? When?"
5. "Will you **contribute footage** for the domain training run?"
6. "Who on your side **owns this going forward** — and do we green-light the next phase now?"

Their answers reframe the follow-up. Don't preempt on a slide; let them tell you.
